Apr - Jun 2023 — Teaching Assistant — Department of Marine Science, Universitas Padjadjaran

Sertifikat

Overview

Teaching Assistant for the Computer & Big Data practicum course. This lab session provided hands-on application of theoretical concepts taught in lectures, enabling Marine Science students (Class of 2021) to develop practical skills in Python programming and data processing through direct and guided practice.

Main Responsibilities:

  • Delivered a weekly class sessions to Marine Science students class of 2022, covering in fundamental Python programming (Installing, data analysis libraries (Pandas, NumPy), and data visualization (Matplotlib))
  • Facilitated a smooth learning experience by providing immediate, hands-on assistance to students facing technical hurdles, from library installation errors to logical flaws in their code
  • Designed and managed a final group project simulating a real-world data analysis workflow, where students sourced datasets from Kaggle, performed visual analysis, and presented their findings by a video presentation.
  • Organized and led a special guest lecture by inviting my BRIN internship supervisor to share their expertise on advanced marine data analysis, bridging the gap between academic theory and professional practice for the students

Impact

  • Enhanced teaching and communication skills through explaining complex programming concepts to beginners
  • Developed patience and problem-solving abilities by troubleshooting diverse student coding challenges
  • Strengthened Python proficiency by preparing course materials and assisting with various data analysis tasks
  • Gained experience in curriculum design and hands-on technical mentoring
